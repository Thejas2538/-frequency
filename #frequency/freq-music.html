Albums

Albums are a curated setlist made by the musicians to showcase an entire experience. Listening to albums has become a lost art with the introduction of streaming services, which made it easy to play any song anytime. This leads to people saving favourite songs from albums and throwing them on repeat. Although not wrong, it is still not the true musical experience offered by the creators of this sound. Here are some good albums to listen to from start to finish:


Currents - Tame Impala

Currents is the third studio album by the Australian psychedelic project Tame Impala, released on July 17, 2015.


Marked a major pivot from traditional psychedelic rock to a lush blend of synth-pop, disco, indie-funk, and R&B. Included prominent tracks like "Let It Happen," "'Cause I'm a Man," "Eventually," and "The Less I Know the Better". Parker described the record as following someone who feels they are turning into a person they never thought they would become. Acts as a deeply confessional breakup album reflecting heart-break, maturation, transition, and the inevitability of change.

AM - Arctic Monkeys

AM is the fifth studio album by the English rock band Arctic Monkeys, released on September 9, 2013, through Domino Recording Company.


nocturnal frustration, infatuation, and loneliness surrounding tainted romance and late-night longing. The title AM stands for the initials of the band, the early morning hours past midnight, and amplitude modulation (referenced by the waveform graphic on the minimalist black-and-white album cover). The album features massive global hits including "Do I Wanna Know?", "R U Mine?", and "Why'd You Only Call Me When You're High?".

The New Abnormal - The Strokes

The New Abnormal is the sixth studio album by the American rock band The Strokes, released on April 10, 2020.


Explores nostalgia, loneliness, acceptance of major life changes, and maturity. Tracks balance a longing for the "good old days" with a self-aware examination of past mistakes, faded friendships, and lost innocence. Frontman Julian Casablancas trades lyrical clichés for raw, mature reflections on aging and letting go, particularly on tracks like "Not The Same Anymore" and "Ode to the Mets".

Favourite Worst Nightmare - Arctic Monkeys

Favourite Worst Nightmare is the second studio album by the English rock band Arctic Monkeys, released in April 2007.


The album is much faster and louder than their first record. It mixes indie rock, garage rock, and post-punk revival styles. The band spent more time in the studio to polish the tracks, creating a heavier and sharper sound. The main theme of Favourite Worst Nightmare by Arctic Monkeys is the chaotic and anxious transition from carefree youth into the harsh pressures of adulthood and sudden fame.

Mr.Morale & The Big Steppers - Kendrick Lamar

Mr. Morale & the Big Steppers is a raw, confessional double studio album by American rapper Kendrick Lamar, released on May 13, 2022.


Lamar addresses his flaws, including infidelity, sex addiction, and grief, actively rejecting the public perception of himself as a savior. Inspired by concepts of presence and conscious awareness, the record serves as an exploration of mental health, familial dynamics, and emotional maturity.

Graduation - Kanye West

Graduation is the third studio album by American rapper and producer Kanye West, released on September 11, 2007, through Roc-A-Fella Records and Def Jam Recordings.


The album shifts away from soulful, sample-heavy hip-hop toward electronic elements, synthesizers, and stadium-sized anthems. It concludes West's school-themed album trilogy, following The College Dropout (2004) and Late Registration (2005). It features major tracks like "Stronger" (sampling Daft Punk), "Good Life" (featuring T-Pain), and "Can't Tell Me Nothing".

HIT ME HARD AND SOFT - Billie Eilish

HIT ME HARD AND SOFT is the third studio album by American singer Billie Eilish, released on May 17, 2024.


The "Hard": She describes herself as an extremist who likes intense, powerful, and physically heavy things.The "Soft": She also loves things that are very tender, sweet, and gentle. The title reflects the emotional ups and downs of the record. The tracklist moves between intense, hard-hitting critiques of fame and dark self-reflection.

ASTROWORLD - Travis Scott

ASTROWORLD is the third studio album by American rapper Travis Scott, released on August 3, 2018.


The production features warped vocalizations, heavy rumbling bass, snappy trap percussion, and swirling psychedelic effects. Many tracks utilize sudden beat switches and musical detours—most notably the multi-phase hit "SICKO MODE". The record blends A-list rap features with unexpected contributors, including Stevie Wonder on harmonica, Kevin Parker of Tame Impala, James Blake, and Thundercat.

Wiped Out! - The Neighbourhood

Wiped Out! is the second studio album by the American alternative rock band The Neighbourhood, released on October 30, 2015, through Columbia Records.


The album mixes alternative rock, R&B, trip-hop, and dream-pop. It feels hazy, dark, and deeply melancholic, much like drifting underwater in a pool. Quiet, slow tracks balance out with faster, rhythmic beats. "Daddy Issues": A fan-favorite track known for its heavy bass and deep personal meaning. "R.I.P. 2 My Youth": The lead single that mixes a danceable tune with dark, honest lyrics. "The Beach": A hazy, dreamy song that sets the relaxed yet defeated tone of the record.

Nevermind - Nirvana

Nevermind is the second studio album by the American rock band Nirvana, released on September 24, 1991.


Youthful alienation, frustration, apathy, and a rejection of mainstream societal and rock norms. Captures a sense of being misunderstood and disconnected, echoed in the dismissive, cynical attitude of the title phrase "nevermind" and lyrics like "oh well, whatever, nevermind". Explores troubled, painful love, heavily influenced by Cobain's personal life and breakups at the time.

Freudian - Daniel Caesar

Freudian is the critically acclaimed independent debut studio album by Canadian R&B singer and songwriter Daniel Caesar, released on August 25, 2017.


The album blends alternative R&B and soul with rich, atmospheric gospel undertones, featuring prominent use of backing choirs, warm organs, and sparse yet deeply textured instrumentation. The album is a soulful, vulnerable exploration of the life cycle of love, human connection, and personal faith. Named after psychologist Sigmund Freud, the project examines the duality of human desire.
