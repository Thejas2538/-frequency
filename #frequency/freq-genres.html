Genres

A music genre is a category that groups songs or pieces of music together based on shared characteristics in their sound, style, or cultural tradition.

Pop Music

Pop music is a mainstream musical genre and a shortened form of "popular music" designed to appeal to a wide, general audience.

Rock Music

Rock music is a broad genre of popular music that originated as "rock and roll" in the United States in the late 1940s and early 1950s.

Classical Music

Classical music is a broad tradition of formal Western art music rooted in European liturgical and secular history, spanning from roughly the 11th century to the present day.

Jazz Music

Jazz is a style of American music that focuses on improvisation (making up music on the spot), syncopated rhythms (off-beat accents), and expressive, swinging grooves

Hip-Hop Music

Hip-hop music is a popular music genre defined by rhythmic speech, rhyming, and stylized beat production. African-American and Latino communities created it as a creative outlet and a form of social commentary.

Country Music

Country music is a popular American music genre that combines southern folk traditions, blues, gospel, and western cowboy ballads.

R&B Music

R&B stands for rhythm and blues, a popular music genre that originated in African American communities in the 1940s. Early R&B combined the emotional depth of the blues, the swinging energy of jazz, and the spiritual call-and-response vocals of gospel music.

Electronic dance Music

Electronic dance music (EDM) is a broad umbrella term for percussive, electronically produced music created primarily for nightclubs, festivals, and raves. The music is designed specifically for continuous dancing in social settings.

Reggae Music

Reggae is a popular music genre that originated in Jamaica in the late 1960s. Usually slower than traditional pop music, averaging between 80 and 110 beats per minute. Features a distinct "skank" or "one drop" beat, where the guitar or keyboard plays short, choppy chords on the offbeat.

Metal Music

Metal music, or heavy metal, is an intense, aggressive subgenre of rock music defined by heavily distorted electric guitars, powerful and fast rhythms, and soaring or harsh vocals.

Folk Music

Folk music means traditional music that originates among common people and is passed down through generations by ear rather than written sheet music

Soul Music

Soul music is a popular music genre that combines African American gospel music, rhythm and blues (R&B), and jazz. It emerged in the United States during the late 1950s and early 1960s.
